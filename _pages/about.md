I'm Jonathan (Buzz) Embley-Riches, a PhD student at UCL in the
[Robot Perception and Learning Lab](https://rpl-as-ucl.github.io/). My research
sits at the intersection of robotics, simulation, and learning.

I built **[Unreal Robotics Lab (URLab)](https://github.com/URLab-Sim/UnrealRoboticsLab)**
-- an open-source Unreal Engine 5 plugin that embeds the
[MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco) physics engine for
high-fidelity, photorealistic robotics simulation, sim-to-real transfer, and
synthetic data generation. The alpha is public as of April 2026; see the
[release post](/blog/2026/unrealroboticslab/),
[documentation](https://urlab-sim.github.io/UnrealRoboticsLab/), and the
[ICRA 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14135).

For questions about URLab, open an
[issue](https://github.com/URLab-Sim/UnrealRoboticsLab/issues) or
[discussion](https://github.com/URLab-Sim/UnrealRoboticsLab/discussions) on
GitHub, or reach out by email.
